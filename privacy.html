Your rights (GDPR)

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data. Write to us at the email above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection, Cyprus.

How long we keep data

  • Cookie choice: in your browser, until you change or clear it.
